Logistics Support On-Site at Customer's Location

 

Companies frequently recognize that logistics is not within their core competency. A decision is often made to outsource the function while keeping it physically located at their facility. OEM Logistics can provide the personnel resources and management support to assume responsibility for inspection, kitting, shipping, receiving and store functions on-site at the customer’s location. Our expertise, internal cost management, detailed work instructions, employee incentive plan and fully accountable work processes allow us to provide economical and efficient solutions to these critical, yet routine functions. Through detailed implementation plans, employee incentives and continuous training, OEM ensures that on-site operations are seamlessly transitioned into fully functional outsourced entities.
