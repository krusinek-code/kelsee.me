Schedule Change Administration 
and Order Entry

 

As end user production demands in the aerospace industry change, the requirements must be flowed down through the supply chain all the way to the supporting manufacturers to satisfy end-item need dates.  The Schedule Change Administration team at OEM Logistics supports this vital role by analyzing customer requested production schedule changes for various end-users and communicating the findings to our customer’s cognizant Program Manager and/or Master Production Scheduler.  The findings are jointly assessed to ensure production capacity and lead times will support the customer’s schedule demand.  Upon determining that the change is feasible, the appropriate changes are passed on to OEM’s Order Entry staff for input into the customer’s electronic sales order management system.  The order entry clerk ensures the accuracy and completeness of all requested changes and keys the appropriate data into the sales order management system.  This data is eventually downloaded into the customer’s MRP system. 

 

For our current customers, this service has been a vital and cost effective method to outsource another routine, yet time consuming and tedious task.  OEM’s focus on quick turn-around of schedule changes has also supported an efficient response to changes in  end-user production demands.
