Procurement

As the first step in the integrated supply chain, OEM Logistics can assist your existing procurement group or provide a fully  outsourced entity.  We will provide a dedicated staff of buyers and a secure command center to support your procurements needs.  Our specialists will locate high quality, responsive and cost effective suppliers that support your strategic sourcing plan. OEM’s buyers will react quickly to the demand changes and potential problems that slow down the procurement process, thereby minimizing delays and overall procurement costs.  OEM’s efficient and responsive purchase order placement and follow-up fosters healthy customer/supplier relationships, supports the on-time delivery of product, and satisfies the end-user’s needs.
