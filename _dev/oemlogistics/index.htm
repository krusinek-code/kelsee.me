About OEM Logistics Support, Inc.

 

Corporate Overview

Who We Are

OEM Logistics Support Inc. is a privately owned Tempe, Arizona based company that caters to the supply chain and logistical management needs of the aerospace and manufacturing industries. Our business is based upon the economic premise that we provide cost effective solutions to a variety of material handling and supply chain needs. OEM Logistics is ISO9002 certified and currently performs functions from expediting and schedule change analysis, to receiving, inventory management, visual inspection, kitting, packaging, and shipping of customer and third-party product directly to the end-user. In some cases, we are connected electronically to our customers internal systems and are able to access information pertinent to the hardware we process and ship. We conduct business much like an off-site cell and in many cases are identified as an inventory location in our customer's system.

Company Information

OEM Logistics operates out of a 32,000 square foot facility in Tempe, Arizona. Incorporated in 1998. Our primary relationships are with several divisions of Honeywell throughout Arizona. Our executive staff has well over 50 years of experience dealing within the Aerospace and Manufacturing sectors.
