Administration/Expediting

Communicating with suppliers is one of the most critical functions in the supply chain.  Insuring on time delivery and problem resolution eliminates assembly line shortages and improves customer satisfaction.  OEM Logistics’ expeditors assume full responsibility as the single point of contact for designated customers and their suppliers.  We communicate on a regular basis by phone and   travel to the supplier’s facility as required to help improve on time delivery performance, enhance communication, and foster healthy working relationships. 

 

Open order reports are faxed to suppliers on a regular basis to track actual versus committed delivery dates.  Deviations to the plan are reconciled to support the customer’s manufacturing schedule.  OEM Logistics  expeditors will also attend   status meetings at our customer’s facility   to report the performance of their assigned suppliers.  In addition, OEM Logistics frequently assumes total responsibility of specific customer projects by expediting parts that are warehoused, inspected, kitted and shipped directly to the end-user from our facility.
