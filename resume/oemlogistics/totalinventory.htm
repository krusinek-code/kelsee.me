Total Inventory Management

Today’s demanding manufacturing environment is one of constant cost analysis, continuous improvement and greater resiliency requirements within the supply chain.  OEM Logistics is focused on managing virtually every step within the supply chain cycle. We offer our clients the ability to outsource many of the supply chain functions and provide additional value to their customers while reducing the internal costs associated with logistics and supply chain management.

OEM’s Administrators/Expeditors track product from placement of purchase order to delivery of product, ensuring that commitments are met and all product is on-hand to support the production need dates.  Upon product receipt at either OEM’s location or at our client’s facility, we conduct a receiving inspection to verify the accuracy and completeness of the order.  We then stock the product in either our customer’s inventory system or into OEM’s automated inventory control system.  OEM tracks and updates these inventory reports on a daily basis to ensure accuracy.

Per the customer’s Master Production Schedule (MPS), product is pulled from inventory and staged for a final-out quality inspection, kitted and prepared for delivery.  Following delivery, all related documentation for each order is retained for a minimum of 1 year on site and 7 years off-site per ISO requirements.  This documentation package includes the sales order, Certificates of Compliance for detail parts, First Article Inspection Reports (FAIR), shipper, bill of lading, and OEM’s internal work traveler.  In each case, p arts are individually packaged per the customer’s specific requirements, and when applicable 8130 Tags are issued by OEM’s FAA Approved Organizational Designated Airworthiness Representative (ODAR).


Our customer’s shipper is cut/closed on-site by OEM’s production control administrator and the product is shipped either to our client or directly to our client’s end-user per the need date.  Many times, orders are processed in advance of the due date and placed in OEM’s “early ship” area. This supports on-time delivery by allowing product to be pulled and shipped as soon as the customer due date arrives.  By managing the entire process, we are able to streamline the approach and significantly compress cycle times.  Because this is our core competency, we are also able to exercise greater flexibility to our clients, thus, allowing them to provide a more resilient offering and increased value to their end-user customers.
