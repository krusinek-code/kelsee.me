Hardware Packaging

Direct Ship to Customer End User

 

OEM Logistics packages and ships a large volume of hardware for its customers. This hardware is received on-site by OEM, warehoused, picked, kitted, packaged, and shipped directly to the appropriate destination.  Many singular end items, which are considered pass-through’s, are handled in the same manner.  Pass-through’s are considered any product purchased by our customer from outside suppliers, received on-site at OEM Logistics, warehoused, inspected, and shipped to the end-user with no further processing, testing, or modification.  This service, which is comprised solely of material handling, has become a cost effective and efficient method by which our customers have reduced the overhead cost associated with processing this product internally.  Ultimately, our customer’s only involvement is to pay their suppliers and bill their customers.  OEM Logistics handles everything in the middle.

 

This packaging provided by OEM can be performed to specification or to the customer’s and/or end user’s specific requirements.  In many cases, OEM Logistics has worked closely with the customer to develop methods of packaging that support lean initiatives at their receiving dock and subsequently in their assembly build cell.  These improved methods of packaging have reduced the time required to de-trash and/or unpack shipments and have supported the build cells by presenting hardware in a manner that facilitates the associated assembly sequence.

 

The method of packaging at OEM can also be tailored based upon the needs of transportation and/or final destination.  For example, shipments that contain fragile parts are packaged in a manner that ensures the customer receives undamaged parts at the final destination.  In another scenario, the final destination may be the customer’s assembly cell.  In this case, the parts can be kitted and staged on shadow-boards and delivered by OEM Logistics directly to the appropriate assembly cell on an as-needed basis.
