Kanban - Supermarket

 

Many customers utilize common hardware (C-part) Kanban inventory locations within their assembly areas. These point-of-use inventory locations contain high-usage/low-value parts. OEM Logistics provides the process and personnel resources required to procure hardware, monitor usage, scan and maintain stock at these point-of-use locations.

 

OEM Logistics goes on-site at the customer’s location to monitor and scan all front (active) bin stock outs and will deliver the necessary hardware to the customer’s site as early as the same day. Concurrent with this scanning/stocking activity, OEM Logistics initiates an order to its hardware supplier to replenish the OEM stock.

 

The advantage in using OEM Logistics is that there is hardware on-site at the customer’s location and back-up inventory off site at OEM Logistics. This means that at any given time, there is an adequate amount of back-up inventory available to cover spikes in production demand and eliminate potential and costly line stoppages. This philosophy   minimizes the amount of inventory held by the customer, but at the same time ensures that hardware is readily available to support fluctuations in production requirements.
