Warehousing

OEM will provide 
a forward-stocking 
location. Suppliers
can ship finished 
goods to OEM 
Logistics and the 
product will be 
warehoused and 
shipped to the 
end-user customer 
when the need dates arrive. 
 

  • OEM will hold buffer inventory for many of our customer's suppliers. This gives the customer visibility to supplier inventory levels without impacting inventory costs and helps support on-time delivery.
  • OEM provides warehousing and inventory management either at our customer’s facility or at OEM’s location.
  • Many products that are considered "pass-through" outside-purchase (OP) are also received, inventoried, and shipped by OEM. Pass-through hardware is considered anything that requires no further processing after receipt, can be visually inspected, and shipped to meet the end-user's need date.
