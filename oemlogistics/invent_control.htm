Inventory Control

 

  • OEM provides warehousing and inventory management on-site for the hardware that is processed, kitted, and shipped. 
     

  • Many products which are considered "pass-through" outside-purchase (OP) are also received, inventoried, and shipped by OEM. Pass-through hardware is considered anything that requires no further processing after receipt, can be visually inspected, and shipped to meet the end-user's need date.
