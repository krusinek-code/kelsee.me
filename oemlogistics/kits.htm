Kitting

At OEM Logistics, hardware kitting serves as a cost effective method to ensure customer satisfaction from receipt of the product on site at OEM to the delivery of fully packaged kits to the customer’s production lines or end-user customer.  OEM picks to the customer’s bill of material, performs a visual quality inspection and consolidates/packages the kits for shipment to the customer or applicable end user.  In addition, OEM guarantees the accuracy and completeness of each kit by executing a process that contains multiple built-in checks and balances.

In order to support OEM’s reputable 100% on-time delivery metric, many kits are processed in advance and placed in the early ship area until the absolute ship date arrives.  Overall, our kitting process is designed to fit the specific needs of each customer and we strive for nothing less than 100% on-time-delivery, quality and overall customer satisfaction.
